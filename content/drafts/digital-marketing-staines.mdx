Staines-upon-Thames sits at the heart of Surrey and the wider West London commuter belt, with a diverse mix of independent retailers, professional services, restaurants, and growing businesses. Yet many local businesses are missing out on customers simply because they're not visible online.

If you run a business in Staines, this guide will show you exactly where to focus your digital marketing efforts.

## Start with Google Business Profile

The single most impactful thing any Staines business can do is claim and optimise their **Google Business Profile**. When someone searches "plumber in Staines" or "coffee shop near me", Google shows a map with local results — and you need to be there.

Make sure your profile has:
- Accurate business name, address, and phone number
- Up-to-date opening hours
- Recent photos of your business
- A steady stream of customer reviews

A fully optimised Google Business Profile can get you appearing in the local map pack within weeks.

## Get Your Website Ranking for Staines Keywords

Once your Google profile is in order, the next step is making sure your website ranks for the searches your customers are actually doing. Think terms like:

- "accountant in Staines"
- "hair salon Staines-upon-Thames"
- "IT support Staines TW18"

This is where **local SEO** comes in. By optimising your website pages, meta titles, and content around these specific search terms, you can start appearing on page one of Google — bringing in a steady stream of customers without paying for ads.

## Social Media for Local Connection

Staines has a strong community feel, and social media is a powerful way to tap into that. Platforms like Instagram and Facebook allow you to:

- Showcase your products and services
- Share behind-the-scenes content
- Promote events and offers
- Connect with other local businesses

Consistency is key — posting three to four times per week keeps your business visible and builds trust with local audiences.

## Use Google Ads to Get Instant Visibility

If you need results quickly, **Google Ads** can put your business at the top of search results immediately. You only pay when someone clicks your ad, and with the right targeting, you can reach customers searching specifically in the Staines, Ashford, and Egham areas.

Google Ads works especially well for service-based businesses — tradespeople, solicitors, clinics, and consultants — where customers are actively searching for help.

## Don't Overlook Your Website

A website that looks outdated or loads slowly will lose customers before they even read your content. Your website should:

- Load in under three seconds
- Work perfectly on mobile devices
- Have a clear call to action on every page
- Include your Staines address and local landmarks for SEO

Think of your website as your digital shopfront — it should make the right impression from the very first visit.
