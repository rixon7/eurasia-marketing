Every business has a story. Ours starts in Hounslow — and it starts with a frustration that we suspect many local business owners will recognise.

## How Eurasia Marketing Began

When our founder set up Eurasia Marketing, it came from direct experience of watching excellent local businesses struggle online while large agencies with expensive retainers dominated the market for digital services. The problem wasn't that local businesses didn't want to grow online — it was that the help available to them was either too expensive, too generic, or delivered by agencies with no real understanding of or connection to the local market.

We wanted to build something different: a digital marketing agency rooted in the community it serves, offering the same quality of strategy and execution that bigger brands receive, but built around the realities of running a small or medium-sized business in West London.

Eurasia Marketing was founded in Hounslow and has been working with local businesses across the TW and UB postcodes ever since.

## What We Do

We offer a focused range of digital marketing services designed to work together:

- **SEO** — helping local businesses rank on Google for the searches that matter
- **Website Design and Development** — clean, fast, mobile-first websites built with conversion and search in mind
- **Social Media Management** — strategy, content creation, scheduling, and community management
- **Google Ads** — targeted paid search campaigns with transparent reporting
- **Google Business Profile Optimisation** — making sure our clients show up in local map results

We don't try to be everything to everyone. We focus on doing these things exceptionally well for businesses in and around Hounslow, Brentford, Chiswick, Isleworth, and the wider West London area.

## Our Values

**No lock-in contracts.** We don't believe in trapping clients into long agreements. We keep clients because our work delivers results — not because they're contractually obligated to stay. Every service we offer operates on a rolling monthly basis.

**Results over vanity metrics.** We're not interested in reporting on follower counts and impressions if they're not translating into real business outcomes. Our reporting always connects digital activity to the metrics that actually matter: enquiries, calls, footfall, and revenue.

**Transparency above all.** You'll always know what we're doing, why we're doing it, and how it's performing. No black boxes, no jargon-filled reports designed to obscure rather than inform.

**Local knowledge matters.** We understand the Hounslow market in a way that a large agency in central London simply doesn't. We know the local competition, the local search landscape, and the local community. That context shapes everything we do.

## The Way We Work

Every new client starts with a discovery call and a free audit of their current online presence. We take time to understand the business properly before we recommend anything. There's no one-size-fits-all package at Eurasia Marketing — every strategy is built around the specific goals, budget, and competitive context of the individual business.

We work with a small enough number of clients to give each one genuine attention. When you work with us, you deal directly with the people doing the work — not a rotating cast of account managers.

## Working With Local Businesses Every Day

What drives the team at Eurasia Marketing is straightforward: we love seeing local businesses grow. When a client calls to say they're fully booked for the first time, or that they've just had their best month ever, or that they're finally ranking on page one for a search they've been chasing for years — that's what the work is for.

Hounslow and West London is a vibrant, diverse, talented business community. We're proud to be part of it, and proud to help it thrive.
