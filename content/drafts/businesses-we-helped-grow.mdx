Numbers and strategies are important, but what most business owners really want to know is: does this actually work for businesses like mine?

The short answer is yes. Here are five examples of real-world results we've achieved for businesses across West London. The details have been kept general to protect client confidentiality, but the outcomes are genuine.

## 1. A Family Restaurant in Hounslow

**The challenge:** A well-established family-run restaurant in central Hounslow had been serving the local community for over a decade, but their online presence hadn't kept pace with their reputation. They had a Google Business Profile that hadn't been updated in two years, fewer than 10 reviews, and a social media presence that had gone quiet. New residents weren't finding them — and they were losing lunchtime and weeknight covers to newer competitors.

**What we did:** We rebuilt and fully optimised their Google Business Profile, added updated photos, corrected their opening hours, and set up a steady flow of fresh posts. We launched an Instagram account with consistent content showcasing their food and atmosphere. A targeted Google review campaign — combining in-restaurant QR codes and follow-up texts — helped them accumulate over 60 new reviews within three months.

**The result:** They went from page two of local search results to the top three in the local pack within four months. Covers increased noticeably, and they now regularly receive enquiries for private dining bookings through Google directly.

## 2. A Solicitor in Brentford

**The challenge:** A small solicitors' firm in Brentford was almost entirely reliant on word-of-mouth referrals. Their website was outdated, slow, and had barely any content relevant to their core services. They weren't appearing in Google searches for their key practice areas, and they had no way of reaching potential clients who were actively searching for legal help in their area.

**What we did:** We redesigned their website with clear service pages, strong calls to action, and content written to rank for local legal search terms. We implemented a full local SEO strategy, including building citations across legal directories and optimising their Google Business Profile for each of their service areas.

**The result:** Organic website traffic increased by over 180% within six months. They now consistently appear on the first page of Google for several of their target search terms. New client enquiries via the website — previously near zero — now account for a meaningful share of their monthly intake.

## 3. A Beauty Salon in Feltham

**The challenge:** A beauty salon in Feltham had a loyal clientele but was struggling to attract new customers, particularly younger clients. Their Instagram account was inconsistent and their Google Ads had been running without proper targeting or tracking — spending money without clear evidence of results.

**What we did:** We overhauled their Instagram strategy with a consistent visual identity, a regular posting schedule, and a content mix of treatment showcases, before-and-afters, and client testimonials. We rebuilt their Google Ads campaigns from scratch with tighter geographic targeting, better keyword selection, and conversion tracking so we could see exactly which ads were generating bookings.

**The result:** Instagram follower count grew by over 400% in six months, with a noticeable increase in new client bookings citing Instagram as how they discovered the salon. Google Ads cost-per-booking dropped significantly once we eliminated wasted spend. The salon is now consistently fully booked at weekends.

## 4. A Plumber in Hayes

**The challenge:** A sole-trader plumber in Hayes was getting occasional leads through word of mouth but wanted a more consistent pipeline of work. He had no website, a Google Business Profile he'd never claimed, and no online reviews. Competitors with even a basic online presence were picking up the calls he should have been getting.

**What we did:** We built him a clean, fast, mobile-optimised website focused on the services and areas he covers. We claimed and fully optimised his Google Business Profile. We set up a simple review request process — a text message sent after every job — which generated over 40 five-star reviews within his first four months.

**The result:** He now appears in the local pack for multiple "plumber in Hayes" and surrounding area searches. He went from sporadic word-of-mouth work to a consistently full calendar, and has since taken on a part-time apprentice to handle the increased demand.

## 5. A Gym in Isleworth

**The challenge:** A small independent gym in Isleworth was competing against large national chains and struggling to communicate what made it different. Membership sign-ups had plateaued and their Facebook page — once their main marketing channel — had seen engagement fall steadily as organic reach declined.

**What we did:** We redesigned their website to better showcase their facilities, instructors, and community atmosphere. We launched a Facebook and Instagram ads campaign targeting fitness-minded adults within a five-mile radius, using creative that highlighted what made the gym genuinely different from the big chains. We also introduced a referral campaign for existing members.

**The result:** Membership enquiries increased significantly in the first two months of running paid social. Their cost-per-lead from Facebook ads came in well below what they'd been paying for traditional local advertising. The referral campaign also drove a measurable uplift in sign-ups from existing member recommendations.
