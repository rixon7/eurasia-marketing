Managing social media properly takes far more time and expertise than most business owners expect. Posting occasionally when you remember isn't a strategy — and a poorly managed account can actually do more harm than good. Our social media management package is designed to take the entire process off your plate, delivering consistent, high-quality content that builds your brand and brings in customers.

Here's exactly what's included and why each element matters.

## Strategy Development

Before we write a single caption or design a single graphic, we invest time in understanding your business, your audience, and your goals. Who are your ideal customers? What tone of voice suits your brand? Which platforms are your customers actually using? What do you want social media to achieve — brand awareness, direct enquiries, driving website traffic, building trust?

The answers to these questions shape everything that follows. A beauty salon in Hounslow has very different social media needs to a B2B consultancy, and your strategy should reflect that. We document this strategy and share it with you so you always know what we're doing and why.

## Content Creation

This is the heart of the service. Our team creates all of your social media content — written copy, graphics, and where applicable, short-form video concepts. Every piece of content is crafted to reflect your brand identity: your colours, your tone, your values.

We don't use generic stock templates that look the same as every other business on your feed. We create content that is specific to you, your services, and your local area. For Hounslow and West London businesses, that means content that resonates with your actual community.

## Scheduling and Publishing

Consistency is one of the most important factors in social media growth, and it's one of the hardest things to maintain when you're running a business. We handle all scheduling and publishing using professional tools that ensure your content goes out at the optimal times for your audience — whether that's a Tuesday morning or a Friday evening.

You'll never have to worry about remembering to post, and your followers will always have fresh content to engage with.

## Community Management

Social media is not a broadcast channel — it's a conversation. When people comment on your posts, send you direct messages, or ask questions, a timely and friendly response makes a real difference to how your brand is perceived.

We monitor your accounts and handle routine community management: responding to comments, answering common questions, and flagging anything that requires your personal attention. This keeps your audience engaged and shows potential customers that yours is a responsive, professional business.

## Analytics and Reporting

How do you know if social media is actually working? Data. Every month, we provide you with a clear, jargon-free report covering the metrics that matter: reach, engagement, follower growth, website clicks, and any other KPIs relevant to your goals.

More importantly, we tell you what those numbers mean and what we're going to do with that insight. If a certain type of content is performing exceptionally well, we do more of it. If something isn't resonating, we adjust. Your strategy evolves with your results.

## What You Can Expect

Our clients typically see measurable growth in engagement and followers within the first three months, and most report that social media becomes a genuine source of enquiries within six months when combined with a strong overall digital presence. The key is consistency and quality — both of which we provide every single month.
