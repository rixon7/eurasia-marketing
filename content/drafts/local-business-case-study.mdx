When Sana first contacted us, she was running a thriving beauty salon on a quiet side street in Hounslow — but almost all of her business came through word of mouth and passing trade. She had no website, an unclaimed Google listing, and a dormant Instagram account she'd last posted on eighteen months earlier. She knew she was missing out on customers, but didn't know where to start.

Six months later, her enquiries had tripled, she was ranking on page one of Google for her most important local keywords, and her social media following had more than doubled. Here's how we did it.

## The Starting Point

Sana's salon, Glow Beauty Studio, had been open for four years and had a genuinely loyal client base. The quality of her work was excellent — she had plenty of happy customers who would recommend her to friends. But that informal word-of-mouth network had a ceiling, and she was ready to grow beyond it.

The core problems were straightforward to identify:

- No website meant she was invisible to anyone searching online
- An unclaimed Google listing meant she wasn't appearing in local map results
- No active social media presence meant no brand visibility among people who hadn't already been referred
- No way for potential customers to find information, check prices, or book outside of calling during business hours

## The Strategy

We proposed a three-part approach: a new website, local SEO, and a managed social media presence. The goal was to build a complete, cohesive online presence that would work together across all channels.

**Phase 1: Website**

We built Glow Beauty Studio a clean, mobile-first website with a clear menu of services and pricing, a gallery of Sana's work, genuine client testimonials, and a simple online enquiry form. The site was built with local SEO in mind from the ground up — optimised page titles, location-specific content, fast load times, and proper structured data markup.

**Phase 2: Local SEO**

We claimed and fully optimised Glow's Google Business Profile, added professional photos, and built consistent business listings across key directories. We developed a review-generation strategy — a simple follow-up message sent to clients after their appointment — that resulted in over 40 new five-star Google reviews within three months. We also created service pages targeting specific searches like "eyebrow threading Hounslow" and "lash extensions West London."

**Phase 3: Social Media**

We took over management of Glow's Instagram and Facebook, creating a consistent posting schedule of three times per week. Content included before-and-after photos of Sana's work, behind-the-scenes content, seasonal promotions, and local community posts. We also ran a small targeted Facebook and Instagram ad campaign in the first month to accelerate follower growth.

## The Results

By the end of month six, the numbers told a clear story:

- **Enquiries tripled** — Sana went from an average of around 15 new client enquiries per month to over 45, primarily through the website contact form and Google Business Profile calls
- **Page one Google rankings** for "beauty salon Hounslow," "threading Hounslow," and several other target keywords
- **Instagram following doubled** from 310 followers to over 680, with significantly higher engagement on every post
- **Google Business Profile views** exceeded 1,200 per month, generating an average of 80+ direction requests and 60+ phone calls monthly
- **Fully booked weekends** — within four months, Sana had introduced a waiting list for Saturday appointments

## What Made the Difference

There was no single magic bullet. The results came from doing several things consistently and correctly over a sustained period. The website gave potential customers a destination. The SEO work ensured they could find it. The social media built trust and kept Glow top of mind. And the reviews gave new visitors the confidence to book.

"I wish I'd done this years ago," Sana told us at her six-month review. "I'm turning away clients now, which I never thought I'd say."
