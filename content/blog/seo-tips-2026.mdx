Search engine optimisation is more competitive than ever, but the fundamentals still matter. Here are ten tips to give your site an edge in 2026.

## 1. Focus on Search Intent

Google's algorithms are increasingly sophisticated at understanding what users actually want. Make sure your content directly answers the questions your audience is asking.

## 2. Optimise for Core Web Vitals

Page speed, interactivity, and visual stability remain critical ranking factors. Use tools like Lighthouse and PageSpeed Insights to identify bottlenecks.

## 3. Create Comprehensive Content

Thin content won't cut it. Aim for in-depth, well-researched articles that cover topics thoroughly and provide genuine value to readers.

## 4. Build Quality Backlinks

Focus on earning links from reputable, relevant websites rather than chasing quantity. Guest posting, digital PR, and creating linkable assets all work well.

## 5. Leverage Structured Data

Schema markup helps search engines understand your content better and can earn you rich snippets in the search results.

## 6. Optimise for Mobile First

With mobile-first indexing fully rolled out, your mobile experience is your primary experience in Google's eyes.

## 7. Use Internal Linking Strategically

A solid internal linking structure helps search engines discover and understand the relationship between your pages.

## 8. Update Old Content

Refreshing outdated articles with new information, statistics, and insights can give them a significant ranking boost.

## 9. Target Long-Tail Keywords

Long-tail keywords are less competitive and often have higher conversion rates. Use them to build topical authority.

## 10. Monitor and Adapt

SEO is never "done." Regularly review your analytics, track keyword positions, and adapt your strategy based on what the data tells you.
